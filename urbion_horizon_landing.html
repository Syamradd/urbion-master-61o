ABOUT URBION HORIZON

Built from a planner's point of view.

URBION is designed around how planning work is actually reasoned: define the place, inspect the evidence, understand the policy, test scenarios, then make a decision that can be explained.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Planning intelligence without the black box.

URBION HORIZON is a student-led spatial decision intelligence concept for the PlanMalaysia / UrbanMind AI Challenge. It combines town planning logic, GIS evidence, planning policy, scenario analysis and traceable outputs into one coherent workflow.

DECISION CAPABILITIES

Evidence, policy, scenario and action in one surface.

The visual system is deliberately information-rich: map-first evidence, controlled cards, source-aware states, scenario comparisons and a judge-ready decision view.

SPATIAL INTELLIGENCE

Read the site before making the decision.

The command centre brings map evidence into the same workflow as planning analysis. Layers carry source state, geometry truth and provenance instead of decorative certainty.

Current planning-source context can be combined with risk, terrain, geology and mobility evidence where available. Cadastral authority remains explicit when authoritative verification is required.

POLICY

Rules, not black-box text.

Typology-aware planning rules are surfaced with clear implications and evidence boundaries.

WHAT-IF

See the delta.

Baseline, modified and reset states show how scenario changes affect the decision chain.

DECISION

Explain the why.

Recommendation, confidence, risk and next action stay connected to the evidence chain.

TRACEABILITY

Know what is verified.

LIVE_QUERY, NO_FEATURE, QUERY_ERROR, EVIDENCE_GAP and SOURCE_CONTEXT remain distinct states.
