# MASTER-122 — Judge Demo Contract

## Demo contract

A judge session should always demonstrate three outcomes:

1. **COMPLY** — a strong planning candidate with transparent evidence boundaries.
2. **NON-COMPLIANCE** — a proposal that is blocked by a demonstrated control failure.
3. **REQUIRES REVIEW / EVIDENCE REQUIRED** — a case where URBION refuses to invent missing evidence or local controls.

## Required proof

Every demo path should expose the decision, key spatial context, rule/applicability trace, evidence state, recommendation and next action.

This contract is presentation QA only and does not alter statutory planning logic.
