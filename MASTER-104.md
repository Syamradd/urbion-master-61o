# MASTER-104 — Land Use + Zoning Intelligence

Adds a conservative spatial evidence layer for land use and zoning.

- Evidence may indicate potential alignment.
- Evidence is never treated as statutory permission.
- Missing or unverified GIS data remains an evidence gap.
- Planner verification is required before a planning conclusion.

Intended sources: PBT GIS / MelGIS, with future binding to verified cadastral and planning evidence.
