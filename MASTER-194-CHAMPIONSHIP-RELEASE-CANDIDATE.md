# MASTER-194 — Championship Release Candidate

## Product promise
URBION turns planning inputs and authoritative/contextual spatial evidence into an explainable decision-support chain for local planning workflows.

## Judge-visible proof
- Spatial context and map layers
- Live station observation context where available
- Physical / social / economic impact screen
- Policy and SDG traceability from supplied references
- Recommendation with evidence basis and planner-review status
- What-If comparison
- KM/OSC workflow readiness
- Evidence gaps surfaced instead of hidden

## Quality proof
The release candidate is accepted only when the current main commit has a successful parallel CI matrix and successful full regression.

## Non-negotiable boundary
URBION is decision support. It does not grant, predict or replace statutory planning approval, gazetted plans, PBT verification, EIA/SIA certification or authorised agency decisions.

## Next
MASTER-195+ should focus on real deployment smoke, visual QA, live-source adapter reliability, performance and final competition presentation polish without destabilising the locked decision chain.
