ABOUT URBION HORIZON

From spatial evidence to more defensible planning decisions.

URBION HORIZON is a spatial decision-support platform connecting spatial data, planning evidence, assessment, scenario simulation and decision review in one traceable workflow.

Evidence first. Spatial first. Explainable AI. Planner in the loop.

Turning spatial evidence into defensible planning insight.

The problem we address.

Planning information is often fragmented across maps, policy documents, technical sources and development-control processes. URBION brings the signals into a case-centred workspace so users can distinguish known evidence, calculated intelligence and items that still require professional verification.
