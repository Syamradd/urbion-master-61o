ABOUT URBION HORIZON

Turning spatial evidence into explainable planning decisions.

URBION HORIZON is a planner-in-the-loop spatial decision workstation designed to connect site context, evidence, planning logic, scenario testing and decision review in one coherent workflow.

The problem

Planning information is often fragmented across maps, policy documents, technical sources and development-control workflows. URBION brings these signals into one case-centred workspace so planners can see what is known, what is calculated and what still needs verification.

The approach

Spatial context comes first. Evidence is kept traceable. AI assists with bounded interpretation rather than replacing professional planning judgement or statutory authority.

The workflow

One case, one evidence chain, one continuous planning journey.
