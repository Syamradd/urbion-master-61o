ABOUT URBION HORIZON · PLANMalaysia / UrbanMind AI Challenge

From spatial evidence to better planning decisions.

URBION HORIZON is a GIS-first planning decision-support platform that connects site context, policy intelligence, evidence provenance, scenario analysis and planner-led decision support in one continuous workflow.

Why URBION exists

Planning information is often fragmented across maps, policy documents, technical portals and development-control workflows. URBION brings those signals together so a planner can see what is known, what is calculated, what is missing and what still needs professional verification.
