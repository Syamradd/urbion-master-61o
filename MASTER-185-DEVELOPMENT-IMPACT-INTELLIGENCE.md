# MASTER-185 — DEVELOPMENT IMPACT INTELLIGENCE

## Objective
Create an evidence-aware LCP-style screening layer for proposed development impacts across physical, social and economic dimensions.

## Flow
DEVELOPMENT PROFILE → IMPACT METRICS → PLANNING ISSUES → POLICY LINKS → RECOMMENDATION

## Physical
Development intensity, movement demand, road/access context and flood exposure.

## Social
Additional population, residential units and community-facility accessibility.

## Economic
Employment input, commercial floor area and development scale.

## Evidence discipline
Explicit inputs may be calculated and labelled `CALCULATED`. Missing inputs become `UNVERIFIED` / `REVIEW_REQUIRED`; the engine does not invent demographic, traffic, economic or environmental values.

## Boundary
This is planning decision support, not a statutory SIA, EIA, LCP certification, engineering assessment or development approval.
