# MASTER-117 — Judge Flow

## Championship demo sequence

1. **Decision Dashboard** — establish the site assessment and planning decision.
2. **GIS Intelligence** — show parcel, land-use, zoning and TOD spatial layers with evidence limitations clearly disclosed.
3. **Evidence Workspace** — show which sources are decision-safe versus evidence gaps.
4. **What-If / Intervention Lab** — demonstrate deterministic scenario comparison and proposal improvement.
5. **Decision Center / Planner Workspace** — close with ranked pathway, evidence gaps and planner-facing next actions.

## Judge message

URBION is a planning decision-support workflow: it connects site context, policy applicability, compliance, suitability, spatial evidence and scenario testing into one traceable decision path. It does not invent statutory controls, and missing or unverified GIS evidence remains an explicit evidence gap.
