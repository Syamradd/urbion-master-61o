# MASTER-105 — TOD + Spatial Analytics

Adds deterministic spatial proximity analytics for TOD catchments.

- Calculates site-to-TOD distance using haversine geometry.
- Classifies analytical bands at 400m, 800m, or outside 800m.
- Exposes spatial evidence as an analytical signal.
- Does not by itself establish statutory compliance or approval.
- Authoritative planning rules and verified evidence remain controlling.
