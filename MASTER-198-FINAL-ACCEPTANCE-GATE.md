# MASTER-198 — Final Acceptance Gate

1. Core deterministic rules

3. Development impact intelligence
4. Policy / SDG traceability
5. Recommendation trace
6. Integrated LCP surface
7. Red-team integrity

## Championship condition
The release candidate is accepted only from the current main commit after every parallel lane and the full regression job are successful.

## Visual condition
Judge-facing surfaces must be readable, navigable, same-origin, and explicit about evidence and statutory boundaries.

## Final boundary
No fabricated source values. No guessed policy clauses. No statutory approval implication. Planner/PBT/authorised-agency review remains the final authority.
