# MASTER-197 — CHAMPIONSHIP PRESENTATION READY

## 90-second narrative
**1. Input** — select a real site or upload spatial input.
**2. Understand** — URBION reads spatial context, planning layers and available live observations.
**3. Measure** — spatial metrics and physical/social/economic impact indicators are calculated only from available inputs.
**4. Explain** — issues are connected to supplied policy, strategy, guideline and SDG references.
**5. Recommend** — URBION produces traceable planning actions with planner-review status.
**6. Compare** — What-If shows how an alternative changes the decision picture.
**7. Review** — Decision Center and LCP Intelligence expose evidence, gaps and trace.
**8. Act** — Planner Review and KM/OSC organise the next workflow step without claiming approval.

## Judge emphasis
The differentiator is not a chatbot alone. It is the connected evidence chain: **site → spatial evidence → live/context data → impact → issue → policy/SDG → recommendation → scenario → decision → planner action**.

## Visual rule
Keep the live demo focused: one real site, one map story, one evidence story, one impact story, one policy trace, one recommendation, one What-If delta and one planner action.

## Safety rule
Never replace an unavailable source with a guessed value. Never present a planning-support recommendation as statutory approval.
